SU10-Kit-Builder

Put WAV files on the SU10's pads, check how they sound, and save a bulk dump you can send to the sampler. You can also open an existing dump to see and play what's on it.

EXAMPLE KIT0 PADS

Drop a WAV on a pad to put it there. Drop several WAVs at once to fill the empty pads in order. Drop a .syx dump anywhere to open it.

Machine settings

These are stored in the dump. The device number must match the SU10's DevNum, or it ignores the dump.

Files

The kit zip holds _header.syx and _data.syx. Send the header, wait until the SU10's MIDI light goes off, then send the data (pause 110–150 ms between messages).

Prototype. Not affiliated with Yamaha. Audio is converted to the SU10's 4-bit IMA ADPCM; previews play exactly what the sampler will play.
